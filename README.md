# Neonatal-medicine-learning-portfolio-

## About

This repository documents my clinical learning, evidence review and selected projects during my neonatal unit internship rotation.

## Areas of focus 

 - Neonatal assessment 
 - Neonatal resuscitation 
 - Neonatal respiratory conditions
 - Neonatal infections
 - Neonatal jaundice 
 - Neonatal fluids and feeding 
 - Neonatal pharmacology 
 - Clinical calculations 
 - Evidence based clinical practice 
 - Clinical research and audit 

## Purpose

The purpose of this portfolio is to systematically document clinical questions, evidence-based learning, clinical calculations,and small research and quality improvement projects encountered during my neonatal unit rotation.

##Note

All clinical examples will be completely de-identified. No patient names, hospital numbers, dates of birth or other personally identifiable information will be included.
